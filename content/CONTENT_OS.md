# Last Bench Content Operating System

## 1. Launch objective

Launch Instagram first with a coherent body of useful content. Facebook can mirror approved posts after account access is recovered; Facebook access must not block content production.

First 30-day objective:
- establish what Last Bench is
- earn trust through practical Malaysia guidance
- show support beyond admission
- create a clear path to enquiry

## 2. Content architecture

### Pillar A — Clarify
Explain decisions students struggle to make.

Examples:
- Is Malaysia right for your budget and goals?
- University, course and intake: which decision comes first?
- What students should know before speaking to an agent
- The difference between admission, visa, arrival and settlement

### Pillar B — Prepare
Prevent avoidable mistakes before application and travel.

Examples:
- documents students should organise early
- budgeting beyond tuition fees
- questions to ask before choosing accommodation
- what to prepare for the first seven days in Malaysia

### Pillar C — Belong
Demonstrate the community and onboarding layer.

Examples:
- how Last Bench supports students after landing
- airport-to-campus journey map
- student community introductions
- real questions new students are afraid to ask

### Pillar D — Progress
Connect education to confidence, capability and future-readiness.

Examples:
- building a network while studying
- skills students should develop outside class
- adapting to a multicultural environment
- preparing early for internships and professional life

## 3. Funnel balance

For every 10 feed posts:
- 4 awareness/education posts
- 3 trust/process posts
- 2 community/proof posts
- 1 conversion post

Do not make every post an advertisement.

## 4. Sustainable publishing cadence

Minimum viable cadence:
- 3 feed posts per week
- 3–5 story frames on active days
- 1 short-form video per week minimum

Suggested weekly rhythm:
- **Sunday — Clarify:** carousel or talking-head reel
- **Tuesday — Prepare:** checklist carousel or graphic
- **Thursday — Belong/Progress:** story-led reel, community post or journey map

1. **Who Last Bench is** — "Admission is only the beginning."

5. **Budget reality** — the costs students forget beyond tuition.

9. **Parent trust post** — what families should know about the student journey.

## 6. Format rules

### Reels
- hook in the first 2 seconds
- one problem per reel
- 20–45 seconds by default
- simple spoken language
- captions always on screen
- end with one action

### Carousels
- slide 1: clear tension or question
- slides 2–6: one logical sequence
- final slide: next step or save/share CTA
- avoid dense paragraphs

### Static posts
Use only when the message gains power from simplicity: brand statements, announcements, student milestones or strong single insights.

## 7. CTA ladder

Use the CTA that matches audience readiness:
- early awareness: Save this / Share with a student
- consideration: Comment your question / Send us your situation
- intent: DM "MALAYSIA" / Complete the enquiry form
- active applicant: Check your next step / Submit the required information

Do not push a consultation CTA on every post.

## 8. Production workflow

1. Select one item from the content backlog.
2. Complete the standard draft template.
3. Verify facts and current rules.
4. Review for brand clarity.
5. Approve copy.
6. Produce artwork/video.
7. Final visual and factual check.
8. Publish.
9. Record performance after 24 hours and seven days.

## 9. Performance signals

Track:
- saves
- shares
- qualified DMs
- profile visits
- enquiry-form starts
- enquiry-form completions
- recurring student questions

Follower count alone is not the primary measure.

## 10. Creative direction

- premium, calm and readable
- white-led layouts with controlled brand-wave elements
- strong hierarchy and generous spacing
- human student imagery used with purpose, not as decoration
- Malaysian context should feel real and contemporary
- logo must be taken from approved repository assets; never redraw or approximate it

## 11. Current blockers and operating decisions

- Facebook account recovery is a channel-access problem, not a content-production blocker.
- Produce and approve the first 12 posts for Instagram now.
- Store all channel-neutral source copy so Facebook publishing can be backfilled later.
- Any current visa, fee, university or regulatory information must be independently verified before posting.
